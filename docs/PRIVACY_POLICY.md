IronLog ("the app") is a workout diary. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data we collect
- **Account data:** your email address and password (the password is handled by our authentication provider, Supabase, and is never visible to us in plain text) and your display name.
- **Training data you enter:** workouts, exercises, sets, weights, reps, notes, routines, bodyweight entries, height (optional), and app preferences.

## How it is used
Only to provide the app: showing your diary and progress, and syncing your data between the app on your phone and your account. We do **not** sell your data, show ads, or use it for advertising or analytics profiling. No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are included.

## Where it is stored
- On your device (an on-device database) so the app works offline.
- In the cloud (Supabase / PostgreSQL) so your data is backed up and available when you sign in again. Each account can only access its own data (row-level security).

## Notifications
The app can send a local notification when a rest timer ends. It does not use remote push notifications.

## Retention and deletion
Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete your account and all associated data in the app at **Profile → Delete account**. This permanently removes your account and cloud data and clears the on-device copy. You can also request deletion by contacting [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

## Security
Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Your session token is stored in the device's secure storage.

## Children
IronLog is not directed to children under 13.

# Google Play listing draft

**App name:** IronLog: Workout Diary
**Short description (80 chars):** The fast, offline workout diary. Log every set, review every session.

**Full description:**
IronLog is a workout diary built for the gym floor. Plan your routines, log sets in seconds, and look back at exactly what you did — every weight, rep, note and personal record.

• Fast set logging with last-time performance and one-tap repeat
• Works fully offline; syncs to your account when you are online
• Routines, folders and supersets
• Warm-up, drop, failure, back-off, top and AMRAP sets; optional RPE and RIR
• Rest timer that keeps time when the screen is locked
• Diary with timeline, calendar, search and filters
• Exercise history, statistics and personal records (est. 1RM, volume, reps at weight)
• Progress charts, sets per muscle group, bodyweight tracking
• 200+ built-in exercises plus unlimited custom exercises
• Plate calculator, kg/lb, dark and light themes

Your data stays yours: delete your account and data any time from the Profile tab.
